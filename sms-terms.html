SMS terms

Text message updates for MAC Apartments residents.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Program

MAC Apartments resident notices, sent by MAC Apartments (operated by ACFLP) to current residents.

What we send

Informational messages about your home: maintenance and work-order updates, scheduled service interruptions (water, power, pest control), payment confirmations, office hours and building notices. We do not send marketing messages.

How you sign up

You give your mobile number on your lease or rental application, or to the office, and agree to this statement:

“I agree to receive text messages from MAC Apartments about my tenancy, maintenance and building notices. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”

Agreeing to receive text messages is not a condition of renting or of any service. See the SMS consent form residents sign.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, usually up to 4 messages per month.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile plan. MAC Apartments does not charge for text messages.

Stop messages

Reply STOP (or ALTO) to any message to stop receiving texts. We will send one message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and you will receive no further texts unless you reply START to sign up again.

Help

Reply HELP (or AYUDA) for help, call (956) 238-9914 or email contact@macapartmentstexas.com.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our privacy policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
